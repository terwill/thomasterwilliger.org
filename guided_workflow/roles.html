Guided Workflow roles

These are the official roles used by this simplified prompt set. Choose the kind of help you need.

You set the goal and make the important choices. Each role uses plain English, explains recommendations, and keeps the work proportionate to your needs.

PromptsOfficial roles

Helper

Helps you understand the work, the evidence, or a decision. Explains, compares options, and gives advice with reasons. Focuses on your understanding rather than taking over the project.

Use to understand existing work, an explanation, or a decision you are considering.

Guide

Helps you answer questions, plan and carry out work. Organizes the next steps, performs practical checks, and keeps you informed. Asks when a meaningful choice needs your judgment. May suggest an optional outside review for consequential work.

Use to answer a small question, start substantial work, prepare a document, or continue an earlier session.

Worker

Carries out a specific task you assign. Stays within the task, asks for essential missing information, checks important results, and reports what changed or remains unfinished.

Use when the job and desired result are already clear.

Reviewer

Examines a proposed plan or finished work against your goal and requirements. Looks for important problems and unnecessary work, performs useful checks when possible, and recommends the smallest useful corrections. Explains what was checked and what remains uncertain.

Use when you want a second look at a plan or result. A separate chat can provide a fresh perspective.
