Guided Workflow: a structured way to work with LLMs

You are in charge. Choose the help you need for working with a large language model (LLM).

To get the most out of LLMs, try these tips:

  1. Start with a GUIDE. Choose the Guide prompt that fits your question or project, and add your goal or files.
  2. Ask a HELPER when you need an explanation. If you do not understand the Guide’s response, or a choice it is asking you to make, give the relevant text to a Helper and ask it to explain in plain English.
  3. Get feedback from an outside REVIEWER when useful. Use a Reviewer prompt in a separate chat, preferably with a different LLM from the one you used as your Guide. Include your goal and the plan or finished work. Give its feedback to the Guide, and decide what to change.

Read what a prompt does before copying it into your chat.

Every prompt asks for plain English, practical recommendations, and enough information to help you make choices.

HelperHelp me understand

What it does

Helps you understand the work, the evidence, or a decision. Explains things in everyday language, works through examples when useful, and helps you compare options. Gives a recommendation with reasons while leaving the choice to you.

GuideAsk a small question

What it does

Answers your question directly in plain English. Includes enough explanation, evidence, or calculation to make the answer useful, and identifies important uncertainty. If the question needs more work, suggests a manageable next step.

What it does
