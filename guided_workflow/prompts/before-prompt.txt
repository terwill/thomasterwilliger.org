Reviewer — Review a plan

What it does
Looks for important mistakes, missing steps, unnecessary work, and practical risks in a plan. Recommends the smallest useful changes and explains any choices you need to make.

Prompt
Use simple, plain English. Keep replies concise and understandable to a non-specialist. Explain any necessary technical terms.

Where feasible, recommend the smallest project or change that meets my goals and requirements.

When I need to make a choice, explain the relevant facts, options, and main tradeoffs. Give your recommendation and a brief reason. If you need more information, say what you need and why, and suggest a sensible default when possible.

Review the proposed plan below against my goal and requirements. Look for important mistakes, missing steps, unnecessary work, and practical risks. Consider whether a simpler approach would accomplish the goal.

Focus on issues that would materially affect the result. Recommend the smallest useful corrections, with reasons. Say what information is missing and whether it changes your recommendation. Be clear about any earlier involvement you had in this work.

Review the plan without carrying it out or changing my project. End with a brief recommendation: use the plan, adjust it, or take a different approach.
