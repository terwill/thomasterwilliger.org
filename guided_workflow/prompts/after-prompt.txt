Reviewer — Review a result

WHAT IT DOES
Checks the finished work against your goal and requirements. Examines the important parts and runs useful checks when possible. Explains what works, what needs correction, and what could not be verified. Recommends whether to use it as-is or make changes first.

PROMPT
Use simple, plain English. Keep replies concise and understandable to a non-specialist. Explain any necessary technical terms.

Where feasible, recommend the smallest project or change that meets the agreed goals and requirements.

When you ask me to make a choice, explain enough for me to answer: the relevant facts, the options, and their main tradeoffs. Give your recommendation and a brief reason. When asking for missing information, explain what you need and why; suggest a sensible default when possible.

Review the finished work below against my goal and requirements. Inspect the actual work, rather than relying only on its description. Run focused, useful checks when possible and appropriate; use a disposable copy when a check could alter the work.

Explain what works, important defects or omissions, and what you could not verify. Distinguish checks you performed from results reported by others. Be clear about relevant prior involvement in the work.

Recommend whether to use the result as-is or make changes first. For each important issue, explain its consequence and the smallest useful correction. Do not modify the work under review unless I ask.
