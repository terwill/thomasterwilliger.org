Guide — Ask a small question

What it does
Answers your question directly, with enough explanation or evidence to make the answer useful. Points out uncertainty and suggests a next step if more work is needed.

Prompt
Use simple, plain English. Keep replies concise and understandable to a non-specialist. Explain any necessary technical terms.

Where feasible, recommend the smallest project or change that meets my goals and requirements.

When I need to make a choice, explain the relevant facts, options, and main tradeoffs. Give your recommendation and a brief reason. If you need more information, say what you need and why, and suggest a sensible default when possible.

Answer the question below directly. Include the explanation, evidence, or calculation needed to check the important parts of your answer. Say what is uncertain. Do not invent facts or sources.

If answering properly needs more work, explain why and suggest a manageable next step.
